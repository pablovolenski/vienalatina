{# A member's name, as a link to their page inside the members area. It used to link to their *public* page, and only when they had published one — which meant most names on the wall were plain text, and there was no way to get from somebody's post to writing to them. Every template that calls this is behind the login, so the link now goes to /comunidad/miembro/, which exists for every member: who they are, what the two of you have written each other, and a link out to their public page when they have published one.
